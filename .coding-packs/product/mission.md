# recruitAI-web — Mission

## What Problem Does This Product Solve?

HR teams waste time on repetitive recruitment tasks: screening CVs manually, drafting job descriptions from scratch, translating CVs and interview transcripts, managing interview schedules, and keeping track of candidate evidence across scattered tools. recruitAI-web turns these into structured AI-assisted workspaces with clear ownership, evidence trails, and approval gates.

## Who Is It For?

HR Manager, Recruiter, and Hiring Manager use recruitAI-web to run the full recruitment operation — from job intake through final decision — with AI assistance at every step.

## What Makes This Solution Unique?

AI tools are not standalone modules dropped into a sidebar. They are **full workspaces** with a consistent productization contract: every tool has state, timeline, evidence, approval owner, and a clear next action. The HR team doesn't just "use AI" — they work inside AI-powered workspaces that feel like a coherent recruitment operating system. Evidence-first design means every AI output carries provenance, version IDs, and a reviewer decision trail.
